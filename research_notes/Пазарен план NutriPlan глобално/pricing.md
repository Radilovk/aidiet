# Pricing and monetization of health, nutrition and fitness subscription apps (global, as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-07. Base currency USD; EUR/GBP noted where found. Most consumer price points below come from third-party comparison blogs (often run by competitor apps such as NutriScan, Nutrola) rather than vendors' own pricing pages. Store prices are dynamic, regional and A/B tested, so treat them as indicative. Official sources are marked (official) where used.

## 1. Competitor prices and tier structures (consumer apps)

### Takeaway
Pure-software trackers and AI apps sit in a tight band of about $40–100/year ($10–25/month list, monthly is 2–3x the effective annual rate). Programs with a human, medication or hardware component (WW, Noom Med, ZOE tests, Signos/Nutrisense CGM) cost $10–400/month. The big jump in price comes from the human/clinical/hardware element, not from AI features.

### Inferences
- Typical feature split, based on the cases above: free = basic logging, barcode, calorie goal. Paid ($40–100/year) = macro/micronutrient goals, AI photo logging (MFP reportedly moved scan-a-meal behind Premium in 2026), meal-by-meal macros, no ads, wearable sync. Premium-plus ($100–120/year) = meal planner and recipes (MFP Premium+, PlateJoy). Human/clinical ($120–$4,800/year) = coaches, dietitians, medication or CGM hardware.
- NutriPlan's bundle (exact-gram menus + human dietitian review + weekly adaptation + AI chat/vision + clinical protocols + blood tests) overlaps with three price tiers: tracker ($4–8/month effective), planner ($8–12/month), and human-reviewed program ($20–75/month, the WW CORE+ to Clinic band). A pure-AI tier priced near $50–80/year plus a "dietitian-reviewed" tier at $20–40/month would follow the market structure.
- Discounting is extreme and permanent (Yazio −50%, Lifesum $22–120, Eat This Much 3 years for $59). List price works as an anchor; effective annual prices cluster near $25–50.

### Gaps
- None of the MFP, Lose It, Cal AI, Yazio or Lifesum figures were confirmed on vendor pages or live store IAP lists for Nov 2026.
- No reliable figures for Fastic, Simple (Simple Life) or Lumen in 2026. Lumen's device-plus-subscription price was not found.
- No cash per-session prices for Fay or Nourish, and no sourced typical prices for human online nutrition coaching. Not found in this research round.
- No lifetime-plan prices found for the major apps, apart from Eat This Much's multi-year deals.

## 2. Subscription-app benchmarks (RevenueCat, Adapty)

### Takeaway
Health & Fitness is the best-monetizing category: median download-to-paid is 2.9% by day 35, trial-to-paid 35–38%, 1-year realized LTV per payer about $36, and annual plans bring about 59–61% of revenue. Its weakness is low first-renewal retention. Hard paywalls convert about 5x better than freemium on downloads, with similar year-1 retention.

### Cited Findings
**RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026** (115k+ apps, $16B+ revenue, 1B+ transactions)
- Health & Fitness medians: download-to-paid (D35) 2.9%, top quartile above 6.2%. Download-to-trial 6.9%, top performers above 23%. Trial-to-paid 37.7%, top quartile above 51.4%. 82.1% of trials start on Day 0. 54% of apps use 5–9-day trials; 18.3% use no trial — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- Health & Fitness median prices: weekly $4.99, monthly $9.99, yearly about $39.94. Annual plans bring 59% of revenue (60.6% in a secondary summary). The report contradicts itself on subscription count mix ("68% annual" vs "68% monthly"). RPI is $0.48 at D14 (highest category) and $0.66 at D60. RLTV per payer: $24.23 after month 1, $35.64 after year 1 (vs Gaming $11.22) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [Sahha summary](https://sahha.ai/blog/health-app-monetization-first-month/)
- The official page excerpt also states a Health & Fitness iOS download-to-paid median of 3.5%, which conflicts with 2.9% (different cut or platform) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- Hard paywall vs freemium (all categories): D35 download-to-paid 10.7% vs 2.1%; D14 RPI $2.32 vs $0.27; D60 RPI $3.09 vs $0.38. Year-1 retention is "nearly identical" — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- Trial length (all categories): trial-to-paid is 25.5% for trials of 4 days or less, 37.4% for 5–9 days, and 42.5% for 17–32 days. Day-0 trial cancellations: 55.4% for 3-day, 39.8% for 7-day, 35.7% for 14-day and 31.1% for 30-day trials. In Health & Fitness, 5–9-day trials convert at 46.8% vs 40.4% for 10–16 days — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [RevenueCat trial-length blog](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/free-trial-length)
- Plan duration (all categories): D14 RPI is $0.36 for yearly-dominant apps, $0.18 for monthly and $0.07 for weekly. Market mix: 42% monthly, 34% yearly. Median prices across all apps: weekly $5.99, yearly $34.80 (up from $31.60). Price level matters: yearly RLTV is $62.19 for high-priced apps vs $10.69 for low-priced; monthly RLTV $35.89 vs $6.67 — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- Regions (all categories): D35 download-to-paid is 2.6–2.8% in North America, 2.0% in Western Europe, 2.4% in APAC and 0.7–1.4% in India/SEA. Trial-to-paid: NA 34.2%, APAC 31.9%, WE 29.7%, IN/SEA 15.2%. Year-1 RLTV per payer: global $23, NA $26–32, WE $25–26.6, IN/SEA $14–19. D14 RPI: NA $0.38, WE $0.25, IN/SEA $0.08. Median yearly price: NA $39.99 vs IN/SEA $18.32. Monthly: NA $9.99 vs IN/SEA $3.75 — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- Web purchases bring 3.2% of revenue globally (4.9% in NA, 0.8% in IN/SEA). Only 4.6% of apps reach $10K monthly revenue within 2 years. Median YoY MRR growth is 5.3%; the bottom quartile shrinks by more than 33%. New subscription apps went from about 2,000/month (Jan 2022) to 14,700+/month (Jan 2026) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)

**Adapty State of In-App Subscriptions 2026** ($3B revenue, 16k+ apps, mostly iOS)
- Health & Fitness: highest trial-to-paid of any category (35%) but the lowest first-renewal retention (30.3%). Annual's share of H&F revenue grew from 51% to 61%; H&F is the only category where annual is gaining. Market-wide, weekly plans bring 56% of subscription revenue — [Airbridge citing Adapty SOIS 2026](https://www.airbridge.io/en/blog/weekly-vs-annual-subscription-app); [Adapty SOIS 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-report/)
- Adapty H&F benchmark for weekly plans with trial: install-to-trial 9.5%, trial-to-paid 42.2%, first renewal 67.7% — [Adapty H&F benchmarks](https://adapty.io/blog/health-fitness-app-subscription-benchmarks/)
- Refund rates by plan (all categories): weekly 2.6%, monthly 3.0–3.5%, annual 4.2%. H&F median 3.4% (older table, source unclear). Adapty's Refund Saver recovered up to 73% of refund value in H&F (2025 report) — [Adapty refund benchmarking](https://adapty.io/blog/refund-rate-metrics-and-benchmarking/); [Adapty SOIS 2025 summary](https://adapty.io/blog/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-2025-in-10-minutes/)

### Inferences
- Rough funnel for 1,000 installs at H&F medians: about 69 trials, 26 payers (2.9%). At about $36 year-1 RLTV, that is about $940 per 1,000 installs before store fees. A top-quartile hard paywall could reach 60+ payers.
- Because H&F first-renewal retention is the lowest of all categories (30%), a prepaid annual plan (~$40–80) captures most LTV. Monthly plans mainly suit high-touch human tiers, where the ongoing service justifies recurring billing.
- A 5–9-day trial (e.g. 7 days) is the H&F sweet spot. Human dietitian review before activation fits awkwardly inside a free trial, because it is costly. Options: a paid trial, or making the review start only after the first charge.
- Regional pricing matters for a Bulgarian/Eastern-European core: IN/SEA-type markets run at about 40–50% of US price points. Eastern Europe was not broken out (gap).

### Gaps
- No H&F-specific month-1/3/12 retention curves in indexed text (only the interactive reports have them).
- No Superwall or Purchasely 2026 figures were collected. Eastern Europe/Bulgaria was not broken out in either report.

## 3. Platform fees and payment options (2026)

### Takeaway
Fees fell sharply in 2026. In the US, Apple takes 0% on external-link purchases for now, while the courts work out a "reasonable" fee (Apple proposes 5–15%). Google, after settling with Epic, moved from 30/15% to tiered 10–25% plus an optional 5% billing fee in the US, UK and EEA from 2026-06-30, and allows web purchase links. Web checkout via Stripe (~3% + fixed fee) is now viable in the main markets.

### Cited Findings
- Apple US: the 27% commission on link-out purchases was struck down as violating the 2021 injunction. The Ninth Circuit (Dec 2025) largely upheld this, but allowed Apple to seek a "reasonable" commission tied to external links. Apple's Aug 2026 proposal: 15% for standard apps and 5% for Small Business Program apps; Epic opposes any fee above 0%. Currently 0% on US external links. Apple asked the Supreme Court (reportedly 2026-09-14) to vacate the contempt finding; earlier a stay was denied — [AppleInsider (Aug 2026)](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/08/13/apples-latest-commission-rates-for-external-app-store-purchases-havent-satisfied-epic); [Courthouse News](https://www.courthousenews.com/apple-denied-supreme-court-bailout-from-sanctions-in-epic-app-store-fight/); [Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/apple-scotus-27-percent-app-store-fee-2026/); [RevenueCat on anti-steering](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/apple-anti-steering-ruling-monetization-strategy)
- Apple standard IAP: 30%, or 15% for the Small Business Program (under $1M/year) and for subscriptions after year 1 (standard; see the Apple fee overview) — [NeonPay 2026](https://www.neonpay.com/blog/apple-app-store-alternative-payment-fees-what-developers-pay-in-2026); [ForaSoft 2026](https://www.forasoft.com/blog/article/how-to-avoid-apple-pay-commission-204)
- Google Play after the Epic settlement: service fee 10% on the first $1M annual earnings. Above $1M: 20% on new installs, 25% on existing installs. Optional Play Billing surcharge +5%. Link-out fee 20% above $1M. Apps Experience / Level Up programs: 10–20%, opening in September. Auto-renewing subscriptions are reported at 10% (Coda) — the MacRumors article notes subscriptions are "excepted" without giving the rate. Developers in the US, UK and EEA may use any billing system and link to the web. Rollout: US/UK/EEA from 2026-06-30; Australia, Japan and Korea by end of 2026; rest of world by Sept 2027 — [MacRumors (2026-06-24)](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/24/google-play-store-fee-change/); [Coda](https://www.coda.co/blog/epic-v-google-policy-update-2026/); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/03/04/google-vs-epic-games-android-app-stores-lower-fees/)
- Web purchases are still a small share of revenue in practice: 3.2% globally and 4.9% in North America (RevenueCat 2026) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)

### Inferences
- For NutriPlan (Android APK plus PWA, under $1M revenue): Google Play subscriptions would cost about 10% (+5% if using Play Billing) in the EEA/US/UK, against Stripe at about 1.5–3% + €0.25 (standard Stripe EU/US card rates; not verified in this round). Selling through the PWA/web with Stripe and enabling the app keeps fees under 5%. For a bootstrapped app, the cost of a store-billed subscription has fallen to near web levels, so the main remaining trade-off is conversion friction.
- The EU DMA alternative-terms details for Apple (Core Technology Commission / 5% CTC etc.) were not retrieved this round. Apple is less relevant here because NutriPlan has no iOS app (PWA only).

### Gaps
- Current EU DMA Apple fee structure (2026) was not verified; see DMA coverage.
- Stripe 2026 rates, Merchant-of-Record options (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy ~5% + fixed fee) and EU VAT handling were not verified.

## 4. B2B / white-label platforms for dietitians, gyms and studios

### Takeaway
Nutrition practice software is cheap and priced per professional ($17–49/month), with small per-client add-ons (about $2/client for That Clean Life). Trainer platforms price by client slots ($5–10/client/month reported for Trainerize). That suggests a B2B studio price of roughly $30–150/month per location plus about $2–5 per active client.

### Cited Findings
- That Clean Life: from $17/month billed annually, plus $2/month per client add-on. White-label branding on a higher tier at about $35/month annually (as of Apr 2026) — [ProMealPlan TCL vs Nutrium](https://www.promealplan.com/en/blog/that-clean-life-vs-nutrium)
- Nutrium: $25/month billed annually for a 10-client plan; $49/month monthly for 10 clients vs $39/month for unlimited (as quoted). Others list $28 or $35/month. Priced per professional; a branded client app is included on paid plans — [ProMealPlan Nutrium review 2026](https://www.promealplan.com/en/blog/nutrium-review-2026); [Capterra Nutrium](https://www.capterra.com/p/173803/Nutrium/)
- Trainerize: Basic is free for 1 client. Extra client slots reported at about $5–10/client/month; enhanced nutrition module about +$3–5/client. Another source says pricing is not public. All figures approximate (competitor source) — [FitBudd on Trainerize](https://www.fitbudd.com/insights/trainerize-pricing-explained-what-it-actually-costs-trainers-in-2026-vs-better-alternatives)
- Healthie: no concrete public per-client price found. Priced per provider seat; higher tiers unlock insurance billing and clinical features — [ProMealPlan Healthie vs Nutrium](https://www.promealplan.com/en/blog/healthie-vs-nutrium)

### Inferences
- Studio/EMS B2B could be priced per location (e.g. $49–149/month) plus per active member (e.g. $2–5/month), with white-label as an upsell (+$30–100/month). This mirrors TCL/Trainerize patterns at a premium, justified by AI plans. Variable LLM cost per member (section 5) is well below $2, which keeps the margin.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 prices for Healthie, Evolve, ABC Fitness / Mindbody nutrition add-ons, or Trainerize's official tiers. Vendors often hide prices behind demos.

## 5. LLM API costs per user (gross-margin inputs)

### Takeaway
LLM cost per active user is small with mid-tier models: roughly $0.05–2 per month for plan generation, chat and photo analysis, depending on model choice and usage. In a dietitian-reviewed tier, human review time (not AI) is the dominant variable cost.

### Cited Findings
- Anthropic official prices (USD per million tokens, input/output, Nov 2026): Claude Haiku 5.5 $0.10/$0.50 (prompts up to 100k tokens; $0.50/$2.50 above that); Haiku 4.5 $1/$5; Sonnet 5.5 $2/$10 (cache hits $0.10); Sonnet 4.6 $3/$15; Opus 5.5 $4/$20; Opus 5 / 4.x $5/$25; Fable 5 / 5.1 $10/$50. Batch API is −50% on input and output (e.g. Sonnet 5.5 batch $1/$5, Haiku 5.5 batch $0.05/$0.25). Prompt-cache writes cost 1.25x (5-minute) or 2x (1-hour) input; reads are about 0.05–0.1x. Web search costs $10 per 1,000 searches. The 1M context window is billed at standard rates for 4.6+ models — [Claude pricing (official)](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)
- OpenAI GPT-5 mini: $0.25/$2.00 per million tokens (aggregators, consistent) — [AnotherWrapper](https://anotherwrapper.com/tools/llm-pricing/gpt-5-mini-2025-08-07/gemini-2.5-flash)
- Google Gemini: 2.5 Flash $0.30/$2.50; 2.5 Flash-Lite $0.10/$0.40; 3.5 Flash $1.50/$9.00. 3.6 Flash is reported as $0.75/$3.75 or $1.50/$7.50 (conflicting, unverified vs Google's page) — [AnotherWrapper 2.5 Flash](https://anotherwrapper.com/llm-pricing/gemini-2.5-flash); [Hashnode Gemini 2026](https://hashnode.com/blog/gemini-api-pricing-2026); [PricePerToken 3.5 Flash](https://pricepertoken.com/pricing-page/model/google-gemini-3.5-flash)

### Inferences (my own estimates, not sourced benchmarks)
- Assumed per-user monthly usage: 4–5 weekly plan generations (about 20k input + 8k output tokens each), 30 chat turns (about 6k input, mostly cached, + 400 output each), and 30 photo analyses (assuming about 1.5k image tokens + 1k prompt + 500 output each; Claude images are about width×height/750 tokens per Anthropic vision docs — not verified this round).
  - On Sonnet 5.5 at $2/$10: plans about $0.52, chat about $0.30–0.50, vision about $0.30, for a total of about $1.1–1.4/user/month. With the Batch API for weekly plans, about $0.85–1.1.
  - On Haiku 5.5 at $0.10/$0.50: total about $0.06–0.08/user/month.
  - A mixed setup (Sonnet for plans, Haiku for chat/vision) comes to about $0.6/user/month.
- Against a $5–8/month effective AI-tier price, LLM cost is about 1–20% of revenue. Gross margin after a 10–15% store or about 3% web fee is likely 70–90%. Heavy chat users are the tail risk; cap or meter chat in lower tiers.
- Human review (an assumption, not sourced): at 10–20 minutes of dietitian time per plan and a $25–60/hour loaded rate, it costs about $4–20 per review. Weekly human review would cost $17–85/month, so human review must be one-off at activation (plus maybe monthly) or sit in a $25–75/month tier. That is consistent with the WW CORE+ ($20) to Clinic ($74) band and with Signos/Nutrisense ($129–399).

### Gaps
