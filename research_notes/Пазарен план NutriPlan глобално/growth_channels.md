# Low-budget, high-scale user acquisition for consumer health/nutrition apps (global, English) — as of November 2026

Scope note: research done 2026-11-07, about 19 web searches and fetches. Most benchmark numbers come from vendor blogs (ad-tech, ASO, and UGC marketplaces). Treat them as planning ranges, not audited data. Each bullet carries the date of the underlying data where it is known.

## 1. Case studies of bootstrapped health/fitness apps that scaled cheaply (2023–2026)

### Takeaway
The clearest 2024–2025 example is Cal AI. It used a small set of mid-size creators on reach- or performance-gated deals, a hard paywall, and heavy paywall A/B testing, then moved the winning creator content into paid Meta ads. It reached about $30M annual revenue and about 15M downloads in under two years, and MyFitnessPal acquired it in Dec 2025. Ladder grew on in-house coaches making TikTok-native video plus a web quiz funnel. MacroFactor grew on an existing expert audience (Stronger By Science podcast and community). There is no public hard data on its CAC.

### Cited Findings
**Cal AI (AI photo calorie counting; founders aged about 17–19)**
- Cal AI monthly revenue: about $30K (month 1), over $100K (month 2), over $300K (month 3), $500K (month 4), $1M (month 6). It went from $2M to $4M per month once paid ads scaled. — [NetInfluencer interview, 2025–26](https://www.netinfluencer.com/cal-ai-founder-zach-yadegari-breaks-down-the-influencer-playbook-behind-his-apps-growth/)
- First influencer test: one story from a creator documenting weight loss, showing a meal scan. It cost about $2,000 and brought about 1,000 downloads in 2 days and about 100 paying customers. That is roughly $2 CPI and $20 per payer. — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/cal-ai-founder-zach-yadegari-breaks-down-the-influencer-playbook-behind-his-apps-growth/)
- Creator selection and deal terms:
  - Minimum about 50K followers, but recent views weighted more heavily than followers.
  - Deals were restructured around a guaranteed view threshold or a set number of videos instead of one flat post.
  - Creators had to deliver a minimum number of sign-ups before the deal continued.
  - Yadegari called this a "sniper approach" (targeted creators), as opposed to mass UGC "spray and pray".
  - Sources: [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/cal-ai-founder-zach-yadegari-breaks-down-the-influencer-playbook-behind-his-apps-growth/); [daily.dev / TechCrunch summary](https://daily.dev/posts/when-it-came-to-cal-ai-s-viral-success-a-small-army-of-influencers-paid-off-sys8cgvhr)
- Paid scaling limit: running influencer content directly as Meta ads capped profitable spend at about $5,000/day. Ad-specific, direct-sales creatives were produced separately from organic posts. — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/cal-ai-founder-zach-yadegari-breaks-down-the-influencer-playbook-behind-his-apps-growth/)
- Monetization and retention:
  - Hard paywall, subscription-first.
  - Hundreds of onboarding and paywall experiments run with Superwall.
  - No retention marketing beyond email; retention came from improving food-scan accuracy.
  - Before Cal AI, the founders launched six apps in about a year. One of them, Grind Clock, saw revenue rise about 10x after switching from ad-supported free to a hard paywall.
  - Sources: [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/cal-ai-founder-zach-yadegari-breaks-down-the-influencer-playbook-behind-his-apps-growth/); [daily.dev](https://daily.dev/posts/when-it-came-to-cal-ai-s-viral-success-a-small-army-of-influencers-paid-off-sys8cgvhr)
- Exit:
  - MyFitnessPal acquired Cal AI with "15M+ downloads and $30M+ annual revenue in under two years". The 7-person team was retained and the price was not disclosed.
  - Sources disagree on the date: TechCrunch says the deal closed in Dec 2025; another account says it was announced the following March.
  - One blog's headline says "$50M ARR", but its own body and other sources say $30M.
  - Sources: [ValueAddVC](https://valueaddvc.com/pulse/cal-ai-30m-revenue-myfitnesspal-acquisition-2026); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3098039); [yuanchang.org (conflicting $50M)](https://yuanchang.org/en/posts/zach-yadegari-cal-ai-50m-exit/)

**Ladder (strength-training app)**
- Ladder went from 0 to $1M ARR and grew 500% in 2023, which it attributes to TikTok experimentation.
  - Pricing changed from $60/month (personal coaching) to $29/month (structured programs), set with Van Westendorp price-sensitivity research.
  - It started with fitness coaches who had micro-audiences on Instagram.
  - Coaches had to "unlearn" Instagram habits: they made standalone videos, because the TikTok For You feed shows each video to new people.
  - Paid TikTok ads sent traffic to a web quiz, which captured preferences and helped optimize ad spend.
  - Source: [RevenueCat Sub Club, published Nov 2023, updated Jun 2024](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/sub-club-podcast-greg-stewart-ladder-fitness-app-growth)
- Ladder grew about 2,000% in two years, from 10K to 175K subscribers, by turning in-house coaches and creators into short-form producers. This is a podcast-host claim, not audited. — [Buzzsprout episode summary](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2432582/episodes/16536290-how-ladder-nailed-product-channel-fit-on-tiktok-and-meta-to-grow-2-000)
- In 2025 Ladder was hiring dedicated "TikTok Creator (Ladder Creator Program)" roles, i.e. in-house creators (posting dated 9/22/2025). — [career.com listing](https://www.career.com/job/ladder/tiktok-creator-ladder-creator-program/j202405301108047879190)

**MacroFactor (evidence-based macro tracker, launched Sept 2021)**
- About 82,000 paid customers less than two years after its Sept 2021 launch. — [Fourscore Business Law client page](https://www.fourscorelaw.com/clients/macrofactor)
- Distribution came from the founders' Stronger By Science podcast, a Facebook group and a subreddit. The launch and anniversary Q&As were live podcasts on YouTube. — [Stronger By Science, 2022](https://www.strongerbyscience.com/podcast-bonus-qa2022/)
- Reddit praise centers on the adaptive TDEE algorithm and the Stronger By Science credibility. The main complaint is price. This comes from a competitor's blog, so treat with caution. — [Nutrola blog, 2026](https://nutrola.app/en/blog/what-do-reddit-users-say-about-macrofactor-2026)

**Stupid Simple Macro Tracker (small indie nutrition app, Venn Interactive)**
- Creator and affiliate referral program launched Jan 2026.
  - Payouts: $1 per monthly-plan referral, $5 per annual or lifetime referral. The referred user gets a longer trial.
  - First 3 months: 49,402 enrolled, 668 link shares, 4,742 referred visitors, 151 paid conversions.
  - One creator reached 100 referrals and got a $100 bonus.
  - Source: [ReferralHero case study, Jul 2026](https://referralhero.com/blog/fitness-app-referral-program-case-study)

**Fastic / Flo**
- About 41% of fastic.com web traffic is Google organic and about 39% is direct. This is a third-party Semrush estimate. — [Semrush overview](https://es.semrush.com/website/fastic.com/overview)

### Inferences
- Common pattern: one viral "wow" mechanic that films well (Cal AI's photo scan, Ladder's coach-led workouts), paired with an aggressive paywall and creator video. NutriPlan's restaurant-menu photo analysis and the dietitian-reviewed plan are the closest equivalents to that mechanic.
- Cal AI's first $2K test (about $20 per payer) suggests a small set of mid-size creator tests ($500–2,000 each) can validate the channel before scaling.
- MacroFactor shows that expert authority plus community can substitute for ad spend. NutriPlan's human dietitians and clinical-protocol pages give it a similar authority angle.

### Gaps
- No reliable public CAC or spend data for MacroFactor, Flo or Fastic growth in 2024–2026.
- Cal AI's total creator count and per-video fees are not disclosed.

## 2. Organic short video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts): faceless, UGC and creator-affiliate models, pay structures, formats

### Takeaway
In 2025–2026 the standard low-budget model is a pool of small creators or "ambassador accounts" (often faceless slideshows) paid about $1–3 CPM on views, frequently with a $25–50 base fee per video, view thresholds and payout caps. Cal AI's model was different: fewer, larger creators on guaranteed-view or sign-up thresholds.

### Cited Findings
- Common pay rates for UGC and creator programs (2025–2026):
  - About $1 per 1,000 views is the common headline rate for organic creator content on large programs. — [UGC Roster, 2026](https://www.ugcroster.com/blog/how-to-pay-ugc-creators); [viral.app live UGC rates](https://viral.app/ugc-rates)
  - App and tech brands often pay a $25–50 base per posted video (most often $30) plus view bonuses, or $1–3 CPM. — [Socialync UGC rates 2026](https://www.socialync.io/blog/ugc-rates-how-much-do-ugc-creators-make-2026)
- Faceless, slideshow, reaction and split-screen formats ("ambassador accounts" run by creators for an app):
  - About $1–2 per 1,000 views (range $0.50–3), usually performance-only. — [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/ugc-rates-how-much-do-ugc-creators-make-2026)
  - Canvas UGC itself cites $2–8 per 1,000 views for 2025–26. — [Canvas UGC](https://canvas-ugc.com/)
- Typical conditions: pay often starts only after 200–1,000 views and is capped at $200–1,000 per video. — [UGC Roster](https://www.ugcroster.com/blog/how-to-pay-ugc-creators)
- TikTok's own Creator Rewards pays creators about $0.40–1.00 per 1,000 views. Brand CPM deals therefore have to beat this to attract creators. — [Viralytics](https://www.getviralytics.com/guides/what-is-a-good-cpm-for-ugc)
- Flat-fee UGC (creator delivers the video, brand posts or runs it as an ad) ranges from about $50 to $1,000+ per video. — [UGCJobs 2026](https://ugcjobs.com/blog/ugc-creator-salary)
- Formats that converted for nutrition and fitness apps:
  - Cal AI: a creator scanning a real meal inside a weight-loss-journey story. Separate, direct-sales creatives for paid ads. — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/cal-ai-founder-zach-yadegari-breaks-down-the-influencer-playbook-behind-his-apps-growth/)
  - Ladder: standalone, coach-led TikTok-native videos rather than serialized content. — [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/sub-club-podcast-greg-stewart-ladder-fitness-app-growth)

### Inferences
- At a $1 CPM, 1M views costs about $1,000. To match Cal AI's first test (about $2 per install), the content must convert about 1 install per 500 views (0.2%). A "scan a restaurant menu and see what to order" demo is a natural format to test.
- A hybrid deal ($30 base plus $1–2 CPM, capped) across 10–20 micro-creators and faceless accounts fits a budget of a few thousand dollars per month.
- An in-house dietitian on camera (Ladder's model applied to dietitians) is the zero-cost version and reinforces E-E-A-T.

### Gaps
- No public conversion rates (views to installs) for nutrition-app UGC.
- No verified data comparing faceless and face-on performance in the health category.

## 3. SEO in the AI-search era (AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity citations, "GEO") for YMYL health topics

### Takeaway
Health queries trigger AI Overviews much more often than average. Citations are concentrated among YouTube and authoritative medical sites, and only about a quarter of cited pages also rank in the organic top 10. Condition-level programmatic pages can still win, but only with clear expert authorship, medical review, and original or structured content. Thin templated pages are risky under YMYL.

### Cited Findings
- How often health queries trigger AI Overviews (estimates disagree):
  - Ahrefs: 44.1% of medical queries trigger an AI Overview, vs 17.2% of non-YMYL queries. Its own table lists health at 43%. — [Ahrefs, 146M SERPs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-triggers/)
  - Semrush: 16.04% of health keywords triggered an AI Overview in Nov 2025, and health AIO presence fell 9.58% between Mar and Nov 2025. The keyword set differs from Ahrefs'. — [search summary of Semrush data, via Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/guide/ai-overviews-ymyl)
- SE Ranking studied 50,807 health searches in Germany. YouTube was the single most-cited domain at 4.43% of citations. About 34.45% of citations came from more reliable medical sources. Academic journals plus government health institutions were only about 1%. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-overviews-cite-youtube-health-topics-467628); [WinBuzzer, Jan 2026](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/01/25/google-ai-overviews-cite-youtube-more-than-medical-sites-health-queries-xcxwbn/)
- Only about 24% of healthcare AI Overview citations overlap with the organic top 10 (finance: 11.3%). This is BrightEdge data cited by an agency. — [Pepper.inc](https://www.pepper.inc/blog/ymyl-and-geo-why-healthcare-brands-need-a-different-ai-search-strategy/)
- SE Ranking compared 40 YMYL queries across tools: Google produced AI Overviews for 51%, ChatGPT answered 100%, DeepSeek 90%. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/how-google-chatgpt-deepseek-handle-ymyl-searches/541193/)
- Pepper.inc claims a 93% AIO trigger rate for condition queries, and some of its other claims are unsupported. Not reliable. — [Pepper.inc](https://www.pepper.inc/blog/ai-search-for-healthcare/)

### Inferences
- YouTube is heavily cited in health AI Overviews. Short dietitian explainer videos that match each protocol page (insulin resistance, menopause, etc.) give an AI-citation path that does not depend on outranking Healthline or the Mayo Clinic.
- The low overlap with the organic top 10 means a page can be cited without ranking first. Clear, quotable, sourced answers (with author credentials and a "reviewed by an RD" date) are the levers.
- The 11 existing protocol landing pages fit "condition plus diet" long-tail queries. Scaling to hundreds of thin variants is risky for YMYL.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch the full Search Engine Land YMYL guide (HTTP 403), so citation-share data for ChatGPT and Perplexity specifically in nutrition is missing.
- No 2026 study found measuring click-through loss for health publishers from AI Overviews.

## 4. ASO in 2026 (Health & Fitness) and web-to-app quiz funnels

### Takeaway
Custom product pages (CPPs) can now appear in organic App Store search, and keyword-matched CPPs lift conversion. Most advertisers still don't use them. Long, personalized quiz onboarding (Noom: up to 113 screens) is the category norm. Hard paywalls and longer trials convert best in Health & Fitness, which has the highest payer LTV of any category in RevenueCat's data.

### Cited Findings
**App Store / custom product pages**
- Custom product pages appear in organic App Store search. Keyword assignment to pages arrived around July 2025 (one vendor's summary of WWDC25). About 70% of apps using Apple Ads don't use CPPs. — [Mobile Action](https://www.mobileaction.co/report/apple-search-ads-2025-benchmark-report/ad-variations-using-custom-product-pages/); [M&C Saatchi Performance, WWDC25](https://www.mcsaatchiperformance.com/news/apple-changed-aso-wwdc25/)
- Adapty analyzed 1M+ Apple Ads ad groups. Keyword-matched CPPs got 23% more downloads than the default page, from about 12% higher tap-through and 9.6% higher conversion. The lift holds only if the paywall matches the page's message. — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/do-apple-ads-custom-product-pages-work/)
- Apple cites a 2.5-point conversion gain over the default page. — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/do-apple-ads-custom-product-pages-work/)
- One vendor claims an 8.6% conversion lift from matching screenshots to search intent. Not independently confirmed. — [Stormy.ai](https://stormy.ai/blog/scaling-conversion-apple-custom-product-pages-screenshot-indexing)

**Subscription and paywall benchmarks (RevenueCat)**
- 2025 report:
  - Median trial-to-paid in Health & Fitness is 39.9%; the top 10% reach 68.3%.
  - Trials of 17–32 days have a median conversion of 45.7%, vs 26.8% for trials of 4 days or less.
  - Health & Fitness has the highest payer LTV: median $16.44, upper quartile $31.12.
  - Source: [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- 2026 report (Mar 2026; secondary coverage):
  - Hard paywall Day-35 conversion is 10.7%, vs 2.1% for freemium.
  - Health & Fitness download-to-paid median is 2.9%.
  - Sources: [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [BuildMVPFast summary](https://www.buildmvpfast.com/blog/hard-paywall-vs-free-trial-revenuecat-indie-app-2026)
- Adapty: the average Health & Fitness paywall-to-purchase rate is 3–7%. — [RocketShip HQ](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/paywall-optimization-fitness-apps/)

**Quiz funnels**
- Noom's web-to-app onboarding is up to 113 screens and takes 10–15 minutes. — [RevenueCat teardown](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel)
- Health & Fitness is the one category where about 20 onboarding screens is typical (median 20.5, n=26). Long flows are justified when they build a personalized plan. — [Lazyweb](https://www.lazyweb.com/research/is-20-onboarding-steps-too-many.md)
- Ladder sent TikTok ads to a web quiz. — [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/sub-club-podcast-greg-stewart-ladder-fitness-app-growth)

### Inferences
- NutriPlan's questionnaire (questionnaire2.html) is already a long, personalized quiz. A web checkout flow avoids store fees and works before the store listings exist.
- On iOS/ASO, build one CPP per protocol (menopause, insulin resistance, postpartum, etc.) that mirrors the SEO landing pages.

### Gaps
- No published Noom quiz-to-paid conversion rate. A claim of "more than 10% of completers" was unsourced and excluded.
- No Health & Fitness-specific data on organic CPP results or on Apple/Google editorial featuring criteria.

## 5. Referral and viral loops; community

### Takeaway
Small cash or extra-trial referral programs give modest but measurable results. One small nutrition app got about 3% of users referring and 151 paid conversions in 3 months. Shareable artifacts (scores, test results, plan PDFs) are a theoretical "double viral loop", but there is little hard data from the health category. Reddit rewards honest stories, not links.

### Cited Findings
- Stupid Simple Macro Tracker (Jan–Apr 2026): 3% referral rate; 668 shares produced 4,742 visits and 151 paid conversions at a $1–5 payout. — [ReferralHero](https://referralhero.com/blog/fitness-app-referral-program-case-study)
- K-factor = (download → activation) × (activation → share) × (share → new download). A value above 1 means exponential growth. Generic benchmarks put 0.2+ as worth investing in and 0.3–0.7 as healthy for SaaS. — [Branch](https://www.branch.io/resources/blog/dissecting-app-virality-from-app-k-factor-to-how-to-use-emotion-to-drive-more-organic-app-downloads/); [Fungies](https://fungies.io/saas-growth-hacking-viral-loops-guide.md)
- Reddit: a $2.99 recipe app got 22,000 Reddit views by telling its story instead of posting a download link. — [Medium, Fit Men Cook case](https://medium.com/@andreaskam/how-a-2-99-recipe-app-became-a-top-2-paid-app-in-the-app-store-f7e83abbb40e)
- MacroFactor used a dedicated subreddit and Facebook group around its podcast. — [Stronger By Science](https://www.strongerbyscience.com/podcast-bonus-qa2022/)

### Inferences
- The emotional-eating test result, a metabolic or longevity "score" and the plan-book PDF are natural share objects. Each should carry an attribution link or QR code and a branded visual sized for Stories.
- Offer an extra free trial to the person who was referred (a pattern taken from the Stupid Simple case study).

### Gaps
- No data found on Strava Year in Sport, Whoop or Flo share mechanics. No published K-factors for health apps.

## 6. B2B2C distribution: dietitians, trainers, gyms and studios, clinics, GLP-1 telehealth

### Takeaway
Affiliate rates in nutrition are about 24–25% of subscription revenue. GLP-1 telehealth programs pay flat fees of about $50–200 per qualified signup. That makes NutriPlan more likely to *earn* by referring GLP-1 patients, or to partner as their nutrition layer, than to pay for distribution. No published white-label rates were found.

### Cited Findings
- Nutrition apps:
  - Nourishing Meals: 25% commission for health professionals, by link or QR code. — [Nourishing Meals](https://nourishingmeals.com/affiliates/health-care-practitioners)
  - Your Daily: 25% on the first sale. — [Your Daily, Dub partners](https://partners.dub.co/your-daily)
  - Eat This Much: 24% of sales; app installs excluded. — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/eat-this-much-us-affiliate-program/)
- GLP-1 and telehealth:
  - bmiMD: $50 per paid signup. — [Awin bmiMD](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile-terms/106761)
  - Lotessa (UK GLP-1 app): £10 per approved yearly subscription. — [Partnero](https://list.partnero.com/programs/510)
  - Some GLP-1 telehealth programs pay $200 flat (Fella Health, Delilah, eMed). Unconfirmed. — [BrandID](https://brandid.app/blog/weight-loss-affiliate-programs/)
  - Noom's GLP-1 offer payout: $160 per one listing, $60 CPA per another (conflict). — [FlexOffers Noom](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/noom-affiliate-program/)
  - Payouts are usually approved after the refund window, and clinically ineligible traffic lowers approval rates. — [Affroom](https://affroom.com/blog/best-ozempic-affiliate-programs/)

### Inferences
- For dietitians, trainers and EMS studios, a 20–30% recurring rev-share plus a white-label or "studio" tier (which NutriPlan already has) matches the market.
- GLP-1 users need protein, muscle-preservation and side-effect nutrition support. Positioning as the nutrition companion to telehealth providers is plausible but unverified.

### Gaps
- No public white-label pricing for nutrition platforms for gyms or clinics. No pharmacy partnership terms found.

## 7. Paid-ads benchmarks (CPI/CAC) for Health & Fitness, 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Health & Fitness installs cost about $2–8 across Meta, TikTok and Apple Ads. US iOS costs far more than Android or non-US traffic (North American Health & Fitness iOS at about $20 vs Android at $5.50, per Business of Apps). Cost per *payer* is what matters: at about a 2.9% download-to-paid median, a $3 CPI implies roughly $100 per payer against a $16–31 payer LTV. Paid ads make sense only after funnel conversion is well above median, or in cheap geographies or on web funnels.

### Cited Findings
- Business of Apps (2025):
  - Blended CPI: Meta $3–8, Apple Ads $2.80–7, TikTok $2–6.
  - North American Health & Fitness: iOS $20.00 vs Android $5.50 (3.64x).
  - Source: [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/marketplace/user-acquisition/research/user-acquisition-costs/)
- Apple Ads:
  - Health & Fitness US median CPI $3.77. — [AppTweak 2026](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/apple-ads-benchmarks)
  - Global median CPI across all categories $1.80, vs $4.06 in the US. — [MB Adv](https://www.mbadv.agency/apple-ads/apple-ads-vs-other-app-advertising-platforms)
  - Health & Fitness CPI range $2–7. — [Admiral Media](https://admiral.media/apple-search-ads-benchmarks/)
- TikTok: Health & Fitness CPI about $2–8 globally; Tier 1 (US, UK, AU) iOS $3.50–8. The vendor claims TikTok CPI runs 15–40% below Meta. — [The Viral App 2026](https://theviralapp.com/blog/tiktok-cpi-benchmarks-app-category-2026/)
- Semnexus 2026: Health & Fitness iOS CPI $2–5.50, Android $1–3. — [Semnexus](https://semnexus.com/cpi-benchmarks-app-category-platform-2026)
- Cal AI's real-world ceiling: about $5K/day of profitable Meta spend on creator creatives. — [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/cal-ai-founder-zach-yadegari-breaks-down-the-influencer-playbook-behind-his-apps-growth/)

### Inferences
- Comparing the payer LTV medians ($16–31, RevenueCat) with CPI suggests paid ads lose money for a median funnel. Use paid only to scale proven organic creatives, which is what Cal AI did.
- Tier-2 English markets (e.g. India, Philippines, Nigeria) have cheaper CPIs but lower willingness to pay.

### Gaps
- No reliable Health & Fitness Meta CPI specific to the category, and no tier-2 country breakdown for Health & Fitness specifically.

## 8. Launch tactics: Product Hunt, Reddit, Hacker News, newsletters, press

### Takeaway
Product Hunt and Reddit launches for consumer health apps produce small spikes (tens to hundreds of downloads), not scale. They are useful for backlinks, social proof and early feedback. Press coverage follows traction numbers, as in Cal AI's case, rather than launch announcements.

### Cited Findings
- A combined Product Hunt and Reddit launch produced 338 downloads and reached the top 10 of the paid Health & Fitness chart. — [Forest Giant, Medium](https://medium.com/forest-giant/launching-on-reddit-and-product-hunt-a81044c46110)
- About 75–81 fitness apps launch on Product Hunt per month, and about one per month becomes #1 Product of the Day. — [Medium, fitness on PH](https://medium.com/@edithdomschy/launching-on-producthunt-as-a-fitness-app-the-pros-cons-d19a548e85ba)
- A front-page post with 400+ upvotes produced only 56 downloads (7 Minute Workout relaunch). — [Product Hunt discussion / indie write-up, via search](https://producthunt.com/discussions/our-1-post-launch-experience-insights-and-achievements)
- A solo developer got 94 downloads in 12 days with zero budget (2026 Reddit thread). — [Reddit snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1rw6iuf/snapshots/2026-03-17T16%3A39%3A29.903492Z)
- Cal AI's press (TechCrunch) followed its revenue milestones. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3098039)

### Inferences
- Use "launch" channels for backlinks, social proof and early feedback (the protocol pages also benefit from the links). Do not budget them as growth drivers.

### Gaps
- No 2025–2026 data on Hacker News or newsletter sponsorships for health apps. Several Product Hunt write-ups are undated.
